About 160cc Engines:
Common in lawnmowers and small generators. This is a 4-stroke OHV (Overhead Valve) design.
  • Intake: Air/Fuel mix enters.
  • Compression: Mix is squeezed.
  • Power: Spark ignites mix, pushing piston down.
  • Exhaust: Burnt gas exits.
